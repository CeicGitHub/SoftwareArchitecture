# ---------------------🦂🦂 Scorpion Foods 🦂🦂-------------------

_____**********************************************************Description***********************************************************___________
This is an app for ordering exotic food, such as scorpions in various preparations, allowing users to enjoy our delicious seasoning on scorpions from anywhere in the city.
